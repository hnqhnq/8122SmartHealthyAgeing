# Q3. Should a clinic offer a "biological age" score?

## English

### (a)

Both people are 70 years old. Their chronological age is the same. Their bodies can still be very different.

Person A walks slowly, has a weak grip, and has high inflammation. The biological age may be about 80. This person is ageing faster and needs prevention sooner.

Person B walks faster, has a strong grip, and has low inflammation. The biological age may be about 60. This person has more reserve.

The score adds the current state of the body. Age since birth cannot tell these two people apart.

The right target is healthspan, not lifespan. Healthspan is the time a person can still function and take part in life, before disability takes over. A longer life can still include many years of frailty. The clinic should delay that decline and shorten the years of disability.

### (b)

The score uses four kinds of data:

1. Epigenetic data, such as DNA methylation.
2. Clinical data, such as inflammation, blood glucose, kidney function, and lipids.
3. Physiological data, such as grip strength, gait speed, and fitness.
4. Multi-omics data, such as transcripts, proteins, and metabolites.

We should test the score against function, such as frailty or years lived without disability. Death is a late and narrow outcome. Two people can die at the same age but lose independence at different times. A score that only predicts death can miss the loss of function that the clinic wants to prevent. Ageing is also a network of hallmarks, not one clock that equals death.

## 中文

### （a）

两个人都是 70 岁。实足年龄相同，身体状态可以很不一样。

甲走路慢、握力弱、炎症高。生物学年龄可能大约是 80 岁。他衰老更快，需要更早预防。

乙走路快、握力强、炎症低。生物学年龄可能大约是 60 岁。他的储备更多。

这个评分补充的是身体现在的状态。从出生算起的年龄区分不了这两个人。

正确目标是健康寿命，不是寿命。健康寿命是人还能活动、还能参与生活的时间，直到失能成为主调。活得更久，仍可能有很多年处于衰弱之中。诊所要做的是推迟功能下降，并缩短失能的年数。

### （b）

这个评分用四类数据：

1. 表观遗传数据，例如 DNA 甲基化。
2. 临床数据，例如炎症、血糖、肾功能和血脂。
3. 生理数据，例如握力、步速和体能。
4. 多组学数据，例如转录组、蛋白质组和代谢组。

应当用功能结局来检验它，例如衰弱，或没有失能的生存年数。死亡来得晚，也只覆盖一个结果。两个人可以在同一年龄去世，却在不同时间失去独立生活能力。只预测死亡的评分，会漏掉诊所真正想预防的功能丧失。衰老还是十二个标志组成的网络，不是一只等于死亡的钟。

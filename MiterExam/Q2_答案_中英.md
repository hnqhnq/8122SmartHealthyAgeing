# Q2. Building an end-to-end fall-risk prediction model

## English

### (a)

Information leakage means the model uses information it would not have at prediction time, or the same information appears in both training and test. The test score then looks better than real use.

**1. Post-fall variables.** Fall severity and the post-fall mobility score exist only after a fall. If the model uses them, it can see who already fell, so the test score is too high.

**2. Later wearable data.** People often remove the watch when they are ill. If we fill a gap with steps or sleep recorded after the prediction time, the model sees the future, including days near the fall. The test looks better than a real prediction.

**3. The same person in both sets.** A random split of records can put one older adult in training and in test. The model learns that person, then we test it on the same person. The score is too high.

### (b)

Only about 5% of people fall. A model that always says "no fall" is 95% accurate and finds no falls. So 95% accuracy does not show that the model is useful.

A better metric is recall: of the people who do fall, how many the model catches.

One way to handle the imbalance is to give falls a higher weight in training. A missed fall then counts more than a false alarm.

## 中文

### （a）

信息泄漏是指：模型用了预测时点还没有的信息，或者同一份信息同时出现在训练集和测试集。测试成绩就会好于真实使用。

**1. 跌倒后才有的变量。** “跌倒严重程度”和“跌倒后活动能力评分”只在跌倒之后才记录。模型用了它们，就等于看见谁已经跌倒，测试分数会虚高。

**2. 预测时点之后的手环数据。** 生病时手环常常被摘下。如果用预测时点之后的步数或睡眠去填空档，模型就看到了未来，也包括临近跌倒的日子。测试会好于真实预测。

**3. 同一个人分到两边。** 按记录随机划分时，同一位老年人可以既在训练集又在测试集。模型先记住这个人，再拿这个人来考试，分数会偏高。

### （b）

大约只有 5% 的人会跌倒。模型如果永远说“不会跌倒”，准确率就是 95%，但一个跌倒也找不到。所以 95% 的准确率不能说明模型有用。

更好的指标是召回率：真正跌倒的人里，模型找出了多少。

一种处理办法是训练时给“跌倒”更高的权重。漏掉一次跌倒，比一次误报代价更大。
